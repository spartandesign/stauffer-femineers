Complete-system test

Test the experience a visitor will actually have.

1Set upUse the planned position, power, code, wearer, or visitor starting state.
→
2RunPerform the same real interaction from start through reset.
→
3ObserveRecord function, safety, comfort or stability, clarity, and recovery.
→
4RedesignChoose one high-impact change supported by the result.
→
5RetestRepeat the original condition and compare.
Meaningful does not mean large. Rerouting one wire, changing one threshold, balancing one power pocket, strengthening one mount, simplifying one program sequence, or making one control easier to reach can transform the whole experience.

Critical

Safety, reliable function, code, power, structure, comfort, reset, or a missing required element. This must be resolved first.

2

Important

Clarity, appearance, visitor instructions, repairability, documentation, or rehearsal that improves the presentation.

Extra decoration or features that may happen only after every required test, document, label, and rehearsal is complete.
