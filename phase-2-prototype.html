Phase 2 rule

Small enough to change. Real enough to test.

A useful prototype teaches you something. If the first version fails, that result still counts when you record what happened and make a thoughtful change.

Before touching materials

Begin only when these four things are true.

1Approved planYou have mentor approval or a clearly approved revision.
2Named questionYou can say what the prototype should prove.
3Released suppliesA mentor has verified and released your station or kit.
4Evidence readyYour iPad is charged and you know what to capture.
No approval yet? Do not copy another project or start major construction. Open your proposal, make the requested revision, and return to the mentor queue.

Your Phase 2 finish line

  • Your prototype answered a specific question.
  • You completed and recorded at least one meaningful change.
  • Your evidence is clear, saved, and ready for Canvas.
  • Your mentor decision and Phase 3 next step are recorded.
  • Power is off and every tool, controller, needle, battery holder, sensor, and cable is returned.
  • Your workspace and digital files are organized before you leave.
