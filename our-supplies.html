How to use program supplies

  • Take only the labeled items assigned to your station or kit.
  • Keep small components in the tray, not loose on the table.
  • Switch power off before changing any circuit.
  • Move used or questionable electronics to the “inspect” bin.
  • Return every needle, cable, battery holder, and controller during cleanup.
