How to use program supplies

  • Take only the labeled items assigned to your station or kit.
  • Keep small components in the tray, not loose on the table.
  • Switch power off before changing any circuit.
  • Move used or questionable electronics to the “inspect” bin.
  • At the Future Stamp station, place the blank, planning card, and loose floss in the named bag.
  • Return every needle separately to the counted tray or pincushion; never store a needle in a student bag.
  • At the District Kickoff badge station, use only the pre-sorted tested kit, keep the battery secured, request a known-good swap instead of improvising, and return shared tools before transitioning.
  • Return every cable, battery holder, and controller during cleanup.
