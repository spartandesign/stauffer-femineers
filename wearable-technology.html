Wearable Technology · Individual Project

Wear your vision of the future.

You will design and create two projects that belong to you: a painted denim shirt with sewn LEDs and a programmable NeoPixel bucket hat.

LED Denim Shirt

Turn your future vision into wearable artwork using fabric paint and approximately 4–8 Leyal LilyPad LEDs.

Programmable Bucket Hat

Decorate a white bucket hat and program 10–12 WS2812B NeoPixels by default, or up to 15 with mentor inventory approval.

Keep it repairable: Your hat electronics will be removable. Mentors will prepare the difficult wiring harnesses; your job is to understand the system, plan it, decorate it, install it, program it, and test it.

Your Phase 1 design proposal

  • Future vision statement and at least three visual ideas
  • Color palette and design theme
  • Labeled front/back shirt sketch with 4–8 LED locations
  • Shirt circuit-path idea and battery location
  • Labeled hat sketch with a 10–12 NeoPixel path, or mentor approval for up to 15
  • Button A signature effect, Button B effect, and A+B lights off
  • Two balanced electronics pockets and a removable mounting plan
  • Technology exploration evidence and materials list

You are working individually. Every wearable student creates both projects and submits her own proposal.
