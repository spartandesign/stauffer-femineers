Wearable individual checklist

  • Future vision statement and three-idea brainstorm
  • Denim-shirt front/back sketch, color palette, 4–8 LED locations, circuit-path idea, and battery placement
  • Bucket-hat sketch, 10–12 NeoPixel path (or mentor-approved path up to 15), data direction, three button behaviors, balanced electronics placement, and removable mounting plan
  • Materials list and technology exploration evidence
  • Mentor approval or revision status

Robotics team checklist

  • Both names, future user/community, need, purpose, and three-idea brainstorm
  • Labeled physical sketch with at least one input and two outputs
  • Input → decision → output statement and interaction storyboard
  • Materials list, role-rotation plan, and technology exploration evidence
  • Mentor approval or revision status

Robotics individual reflection

Each partner answers: What technology did you operate or explain? What idea did you contribute? What do you need to learn next?

Final upload check

  • Every image is upright, clear, and readable.
  • Your name—or both robotics team members—is included.
  • Your mentor decision is recorded.
  • You saved the original evidence for later checkpoints.
  • You know your first prototype task for October 5.
