Engineering Design Process

Your idea is allowed to change.

Engineers and designers learn by making, testing, and improving. A redesign is evidence that your thinking grew.

1

Imagine

Notice possibilities and decide what future you want to help create.

2

Research

Learn about your user, materials, technology, and similar solutions.

3

Brainstorm

Create several ideas before choosing. Different is more useful than perfect.

4

Design

Plan parts, placement, materials, interaction, appearance, and safety.

5

Prototype

Make a quick model that helps you answer an important question.

6

Build

Construct carefully and compare the result with your plan.

7

Test

Try the project in a specific way and observe what actually happens.

8

Improve

Change one useful thing, then test again.

9

Present & Reflect

Share the story, explain your decisions, and decide what you would do next.

Today’s focus

On Workday 1, spend most of your time on Imagine, Research, Brainstorm, and Design. Your technology explorations are part of your research.

Evidence to save: Your future statement, brainstorm, labeled design, technology exploration, and notes about choices you changed.
