Learn

LEDs care about direction

Simplified guide — follow the printed + and − marks on the actual boards.

−Holder − pad

Polarity

Polarity means that positive and negative have a correct direction. Match + to + and − to −.

A complete loop

Electricity needs an unbroken path from the battery, through the LED, and back to the battery.

Separate paths

The positive and negative paths must not touch each other. If they touch, the circuit can short.

Look closely: Sewable LED boards usually mark their connection pads with a plus and minus. Always inspect the exact LED at your station instead of guessing from its shape.

Try it

Test your LED

  1. Start with the holder switched off.

    Find the slide switch on the LilyPad CR2032 holder. It stays off before any circuit change.

  2. Find the polarity marks.

    Point to the + and − pads on the sewable LED. Ask a partner or mentor to confirm.

  3. Match the prepared connections.

    Connect battery positive to the LED’s positive pad and battery negative to the LED’s negative pad. Keep the two paths apart.

  4. Inspect before powering.

    Check that the connections are secure, no metal pieces are crossing, and nothing feels warm.

  5. Turn on and observe.

    Power the test circuit for a few seconds. The LED should glow steadily.

  6. Turn power off.

    Switch off or disconnect the battery before touching or changing any connection.

Test

Can you prove you understand it?

  • Your LED turns on steadily.
  • You can point to the positive and negative pads.
  • You can trace the complete circuit loop with your finger.
  • You can explain why the two paths must stay separate.
  • You turn the power off before changing the circuit.

Troubleshoot

If your LED does not light

STOP→OBSERVE→CHANGE ONE THING→TEST

1. Power

Is the approved battery holder connected and switched on? Do not replace the battery without a mentor.

2. Polarity

Does battery positive go to LED positive? Does battery negative go to LED negative?

3. Contact

Is each prepared connection actually touching its correct LED pad?

4. Short circuit

Are the positive and negative paths touching? Turn power off before separating them.

5. Known-good test

Ask a mentor to test your LED with the station’s known-good circuit. Change only one part at a time.

Stop immediately if…

Anything becomes warm, a battery looks damaged, or you smell something unusual. Disconnect power and tell a mentor.
