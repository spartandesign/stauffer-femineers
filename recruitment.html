What future will you design?

Join Stauffer Femineers for creative engineering, digital design, teamwork, and two public showcases. Your participation year assigns your pathway: first-year Femineers begin with Creative Robotics; second- and third-year Femineers advance to Wearable Technology.

No experience required

Curiosity matters more than already knowing how.

You do not need top grades, previous coding experience, or a finished project idea. We are looking for students who are curious, willing to keep trying, ready to collaborate, and able to commit to the program.

Know someone who may not apply on her own? Math, science, technology, art, and other teachers may recommend students who would benefit from the opportunity. A recommendation supports—but does not replace—the student application.

Pathway by participation year

You do not need to choose a pathway.

⚙

1st year · Work in a team of two

Creative Robotics

All first-year Femineers use a Hummingbird Premium Kit, sensors, lights, movement, craft materials, and BirdBlox programming to build an interactive invention.

You explore
Inputs, outputs, movement and programming
You design
An invention for a future person or community
You present
A visitor-ready interactive experience
THEN
✦

2nd or 3rd year · Work individually

Wearable Technology

Returning Femineers create a painted LED denim shirt and a programmable pixel bucket hat that express who they may become or what future they want to help create.

You explore
Circuits, conductive thread, micro:bit and lights
You design
Your own connected shirt-and-hat concept
You present
A wearable story about your future

Both pathways can use Tinkercad.

Before you submit

  • Your answers are your own and include specific examples.
  • You reviewed all six school-day work sessions with your family.
  • You understand that missed classwork must be completed.
  • You provided an accurate Femineers T-shirt size—and a denim-shirt size only if you are a second- or third-year Wearable Technology member.
  • You are willing to design, build, test, document, clean up, and present.
