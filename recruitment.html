What future will you design?

Join Stauffer Femineers for creative engineering, digital design, teamwork, and two public showcases. First-year members begin with Creative Robotics, second-year members advance to Wearable Technology, and third-year members choose either pathway.

No experience required

Curiosity matters more than already knowing how.

You do not need top grades, previous coding experience, or a finished project idea. We are looking for students who are curious, willing to keep trying, ready to collaborate, and able to commit to the program.

Know someone who may not apply on her own? Math, science, technology, art, and other teachers may recommend students who would benefit from the opportunity. A recommendation supports—but does not replace—the student application.

Pathway by participation year

Third-year Femineers now choose their pathway.

1st year · Team of two

Creative Robotics

Use a Hummingbird Premium Kit, sensors, lights, movement, craft materials, and BirdBlox to build an interactive invention.

2nd year · Individual

Wearable Technology

Create a painted LED denim shirt and programmable pixel bucket hat using a micro:bit and MakeCode.

3rd year · Student choice

Creative Robotics or Wearables

Choose a team-of-two Robotics project or an individual Wearable Technology project on the returning-member form.
