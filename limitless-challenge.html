Try this sentence

I want to design a future where…

Example: “I want to design a future where clothing helps people express their identity and feel confident.”

Your task

  1. Write one future statement.
  2. Sketch or list at least three different project ideas. Starters may help, but your three ideas must be meaningfully different.
  3. Circle the idea that feels most meaningful and possible.
  4. If you used a starter, label the three things you changed.
Capture this for Canvas: Save your future statement and one clear photo or screenshot showing all three ideas.
