Tutorials/Capture Good Evidence

Document Your Process

Capture it while it is happening.

Good evidence helps you remember what you tried, explain your thinking, and avoid rebuilding your story at the end.

1

Make it clear

Clean the camera lens. Fill the frame. Hold the iPad steady. Check that sketches and labels are readable.

2

Show the important part

Photograph the circuit, code, mechanism, test result, or design change—not just smiling faces.

3

Add a useful note

Write what you tried, what happened, and what you learned or changed.

A strong evidence note

“We expected the servo arm to lift the door. It moved too far and jammed, so we reduced its angle before testing again.”

Before you upload

  • The image is upright, focused, and bright enough.
  • Names are included where Canvas asks for them.
  • Labels and code can be read when the image is opened.
  • Your note explains why the evidence matters.
  • You saved the original for later checkpoints.
