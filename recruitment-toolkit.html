Club Day

Short recruitment script

About 30 seconds

“Femineers is for students who want to imagine, design, and build—not only students who already know engineering or coding. This year you can create your own glowing denim shirt and programmable hat, or work with a partner to build an interactive Hummingbird robot. We have six school-day work sessions and two showcases. We’re looking for curiosity, persistence, teamwork, and commitment. Scan the code to explore both pathways and apply by September 4.”

Ask

“Would you rather design something people can wear or something people can interact with?”

Show

Use the site’s concept images and technology diagrams until physical examples are available.

Invite

Encourage students who seem interested but hesitant. Remind them that prior experience and top grades are not required.

Club Day table checklist: Table sign, take-home flyers, iPad showing the recruitment page, working QR test, interest-count tally, application deadline sign, mentor contact process, and pathway visuals.

Communication templates

Acceptance, waitlist, and follow-up messages

Accepted

Subject: Welcome to Stauffer Femineers!

We’re excited to offer you a place in Stauffer Femineers for 2026–2027. Our first workday is Monday, September 21, in Room 14 from 8:00 a.m.–2:41 p.m. Please confirm your place by [DATE] and review the family information. We can’t wait to see what future you design.

Waitlist

Subject: Stauffer Femineers application update

Thank you for applying to Femineers. We received more interest than our 50 available spaces and are placing you on the waitlist. This is not a rejection. We will contact you by [DATE] if a space becomes available. Thank you for sharing your ideas with us.

Incomplete

Subject: Action needed for your Femineers application

We received part of your Femineers application, but still need [ITEM]. Please submit it by [DATE] so we can complete the review. If you need help or a paper copy, contact [SCHOOL CONTACT METHOD].

Teacher invitation

Subject: Help us identify future Femineers

Please recommend students who show curiosity, persistence, creativity, collaboration, or who would benefit from a hands-on engineering community—including students who may not nominate themselves. Recommendations support the application and are not grade checks.

Confirmation reminder

Subject: Please confirm your Femineers place

We offered you a place in Femineers and need your response by [DATE]. If we do not receive confirmation, we may offer the place to a student on the waitlist. Please reply through [SCHOOL-APPROVED METHOD].

Not placed

Subject: Thank you for applying to Femineers

Thank you for the care you put into your application. We were not able to offer you one of this year’s limited spaces. This decision does not measure your potential as a designer or engineer. We hope you continue exploring STEM opportunities and consider applying again.

Before sending: Replace every bracketed placeholder, double-check names and decisions against the private tracker, use blind copy for group messages when appropriate, and follow school communication rules.
