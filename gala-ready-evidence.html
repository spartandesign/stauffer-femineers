Checkpoint 4 is an operations plan, not only a beauty photo. Another mentor should be able to identify the project, verify its status, find its code and supplies, set it up, understand the demonstration, and shut it down safely.

Required final record

Complete all six proof areas.

Proof areaWhat to submitWhat it must show
1 · Final projectClear overall and labeled close-up photos.Required technology, final appearance, safe power/component access, project identity, and presentation setup.
2 · InspectionMentor form or recorded status.Safety, function, comfort/stability, repairability, presentation, and any modified feature.
3 · ReliabilityTwo-trial record plus one complete video.Setup, complete demonstration, reset, repeatability, shutdown, and no hidden mentor rescue.
4 · Engineering storyFinal project card and 45-second explanation.Future user/need, purpose, technology, testing evidence, redesign, and retest result.
5 · RecoveryWritten safe backup or modified demonstration.First stop action, feature removed from use, static/video alternative, and mentor-only repair boundary.
6 · LogisticsSetup/pack-down photo and checklist.Owner labels, code name, supplies, power state, container/rack, sign, spares, and inventory.

45-second explanation

Write it, practice it, then speak naturally.

Future: “I/we designed _____ for _____ because _____.”

Technology: “It uses _____ to _____.”

Test: “During testing, I/we observed _____.”

Redesign: “That evidence led me/us to change _____.”

Retest: “The second test showed _____.”

Invitation: “Please _____ / notice _____.”

Safe backup plan

Write what happens if the live feature cannot continue.

Failure sign: I/we will stop if _____.

First safe action: I/we will _____.

Feature removed from use: _____ will remain off/still.

Audience alternative: I/we can use the project card, static display, explanation, or evidence video to show _____.

Only a mentor may: _____.

Never hide a known safety problem. A modified demonstration can still show excellent engineering when the limitation, evidence, and safe decision are explained honestly.

Canvas evidence

Checkpoint 4: Gala Ready

  • Final photos clearly show the complete project and required details.
  • Mentor inspection status is included.
  • Two reliability trials passed, or the remaining named repair and retest plan are recorded.
  • The complete demonstration video includes setup state, operation, reset, and safe shutdown.
  • The project card explains the future need, purpose, technology, testing, and redesign.
  • The safe backup or modified demonstration is specific.
  • Wearable evidence includes fashion-show rehearsal; robotics evidence includes both partner roles.
  • Setup and pack-down use labeled items and a complete inventory.
  • Final code is backed up and named.
  • The project status is Gala-ready, named repair, or modified demonstration.
