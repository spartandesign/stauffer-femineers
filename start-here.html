Welcome, future designer.

Today is about finding a future you care about, exploring your technology, and creating a plan that you can really build.

What future do you want to help design?

Your finish line for today

  • Choose a future focus and create at least three ideas.
  • Complete the required technology explorations for your pathway.
  • Finish your pathway design proposal.
  • Explain your plan and receive a mentor decision.
  • Submit clear evidence to Canvas Checkpoint 1.
