Optional digital fabrication

Need a custom part?

Use Tinkercad in Phase 1 to create a digital concept. If you later request a 3D-printed, laser-cut, or X-Carve part, this concept becomes the starting point for your Phase 2 STL or SVG file check.

Open the Fabrication Lab guide
This is not required for every project. Use confirmed program materials when they already solve the problem. Create a custom part only when it has a clear purpose.

Your finish line for today

  • Choose a future focus and create at least three ideas within your assigned pathway.
  • Complete the required technology explorations for your assigned pathway.
  • If your idea needs a custom fabricated part, save a Tinkercad concept, screenshot, dimensions, and planned STL or SVG output.
  • Finish your assigned pathway design proposal.
  • Explain your plan and receive a mentor decision.
  • Submit clear evidence to Canvas Checkpoint 1.
Important: Today is for imagining, exploring, and planning. Do not begin major construction or operate fabrication machines until your design is approved.
