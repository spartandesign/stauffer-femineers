Family Information and Commitment

Recruitment: August 18–September 4, 2026

Pathway placement by participation year

First-year Femineers complete Creative Robotics in teams of two. Second-year Femineers complete Wearable Technology individually. Third-year Femineers choose Creative Robotics or Wearable Technology. Every member will imagine, plan, prototype, build, program, test, document, improve, and present a future-focused project. No previous engineering or coding experience is required.
