Milestone 4 · Build Approval

Explain your plan. Get ready to build.

Mentor approval is a quick design conversation. It helps make sure your project is meaningful, complete, safe, possible, and ready for the next workday.

Before joining the approval queue

  • Complete every item on your pathway proposal.
  • Use labels that another person can understand.
  • Check that your plan uses confirmed materials and equipment.
  • Practice explaining what your project does and why it matters.
  • Robotics: both partners must be able to explain the full project.
