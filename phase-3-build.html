Phase 3 rule

Build the approved plan. Keep every important part repairable.

This is the largest construction day, but progress does not mean covering mistakes or sealing electronics inside a project. Strong work can still be inspected, tested, repaired, and explained.

Before touching final materials

Begin major construction only when these four things are true.

Your Phase 3 finish line

  • Your approved major-build milestone is complete or safely paused.
  • Permanent work matches the plan or has a documented reason for changing.
  • Your overall photo, close-up, test result, and next-step note are saved.
  • Your mentor status and first December test or repair are recorded.
  • Wet paint is flat in the drying area; needles and tools are counted.
  • Every controller is off, every robotics kit is counted, and every loose part is labeled.
