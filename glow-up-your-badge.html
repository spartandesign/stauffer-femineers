Glow Up Your Badge.

Create a personalized light-up wearable badge while discovering what a battery, LED, and complete circuit can do. This is a theme-neutral district activity for Femineers from every participating school.

Prototype status — not yet student-release ready: The final lighting module, battery, conductive connection, orientation marks, and placement photo must be added after the real badge prototype passes timing, safety, and reliability testing. Until then, use this page for planning only—not as a wiring guide.

How it works

The light needs one complete path.

BATTERY PROVIDES ENERGY→PATH IS COMPLETE→LED LIGHTS

The battery supplies energy and the LED produces light. If the final kit has marked positive and negative sides, the LED must face the direction shown on the station card. Test the circuit before closing the badge.

Build it · 10–12 minutes

Personalize, connect, test, and wear.

  1. Choose your badge insert.

    Take one base or insert from your kit and keep the electronic parts in the tray.

  2. Personalize the design.

    Add your name, colors, symbols, or shapes without covering the marked circuit area.

  3. Place the light.

    Install the mentor-tested LED or light module exactly where the final station card shows.

  4. Add the power module.

    Keep the battery secure and follow any marked positive/negative orientation. Use no solder and only the kit provided.

  5. Complete the approved connection.

    Close or attach the no-solder circuit connection shown in the demonstration. Do not improvise a different circuit.

  6. Test before closing.

    Switch on or complete the path. If the badge stays dark, use the quick check below and ask a mentor.

  7. Finish and wear.

    Secure the badge and attachment. Wear it only after a mentor confirms that the light, battery, and attachment are safe.

Troubleshoot

If the LED does not light

CHECK BATTERY→CHECK POLARITY→CHECK CONNECTION→ASK FOR A SWAP

1 · Battery

Is it seated and switched on or connected exactly as demonstrated?

2 · LED direction

If the kit has polarity marks, do the positive and negative sides match the station card?

3 · Complete path

Are all approved contact points touching securely? Do not add loose metal or another battery.

4 · Quick component swap

Ask a mentor for a known-good battery or LED. The station will swap questionable parts instead of spending several minutes on one badge.

Stop and tell a mentor: damaged or warm battery, exposed sharp part, torn wire or conductive material, loose power module, unstable attachment, unusual odor, or a circuit that works only when squeezed hard.

Your badge is ready when…

  • The LED lights.
  • You can identify the battery and LED.
  • You can explain that the circuit needs a complete path.
  • The battery, connections, and wearable attachment are secure.
  • You returned shared tools and scraps.
