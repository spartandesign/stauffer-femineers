Failure-recovery plan

Know what to do without improvising around power.

Shirt light fails

Switch off, remove/isolate the battery as directed, stop the live demonstration, and use the design explanation or approved static display. Do not sew or press connections while powered.

Hat pattern fails

Use the saved working program only if a mentor approves. Otherwise switch off the strip and explain the intended pattern with the evidence video.

Comfort or fit fails

Remove the item and present it on the stand. Never continue wearing a project that pinches, shifts, blocks vision, smells unusual, or feels warm.

Power or connection concern

Switch both supplies off, remove the project from public use, and call a mentor. Do not open, reconnect, substitute, or rebuild the harness at the Gala.

Fashion-show rehearsal

Practice the same sequence you will use in the Library.

  1. Prepare offstage.

    Complete the stand function test, verify both garments and labels, secure hair/clothing, and ask for mentor clearance before wearing.

  2. Enter and pause.

    Walk at the approved pace, stop at the marked point, face the audience, and allow time for people to see the shirt and hat.

  3. Operate on cue.

    Use the planned button state without blocking vision or reaching into an unsafe position. If it fails, follow the backup plan calmly.

  4. Share the engineering story.

    In about 45 seconds, name the future vision, technology, test evidence, and redesign. Answer one practice question.

  5. Exit and shut down.

    Move to the safe area, switch off exactly as directed, inspect for shifting or warmth, and return the garments to the assigned rack/stand.

Rehearse in the actual order. Practice the transition before and after your turn so the fashion show does not depend on last-second power, missing garments, unlabeled batteries, or mentor rescue.
